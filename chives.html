What's for dinner?

Less deciding, more enjoying.

Chives turns the dinners you already make into a varied week, so you stop asking the same question every night.

Plan the Week in One Tap

Generate seven dinners, Monday through Sunday. Chives avoids repeats and spreads similar meals apart so the week never feels like a rerun.

Lock What You Love

Lock the days you're happy with and regenerate the rest. Hold and drag a dinner to move it to another day when plans change.

Your Rotation, Your Way

Keep a list of go-to dinners sorted by category, from Chicken and Pasta to Mexican and Vegetarian, so every week stays varied.

Recipes You Can Cook From

Write recipes with servings and ingredient amounts, add sides and desserts to any day, and cook from a clean view that keeps the screen on.

A Shopping List That Adds Up

Build a list from the week's meals and sides with matching ingredients combined. Tick items off as you shop or share what's left.

Import Any Recipe

Bring in a recipe from a link, pasted text, or a photo, then review the ingredients and steps before you save it.
